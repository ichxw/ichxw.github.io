I am interested in structural biology, biomolecular modeling, and computational approaches to molecular recognition.

My work focuses on AI-driven and physics-based drug discovery, including CADD/AIDD methods for small molecules, antibodies, and antibody–drug conjugates (ADCs).

This site is a place for:

- blog-style scientific essays
- technical notes and analysis
- perspectives on modeling, data science, and AI in molecular discovery
- figure-rich posts with images organized cleanly by article

## Topics

- structural biology and molecular recognition
- small-molecule drug discovery and CADD
- antibodies and antibody–drug conjugates (ADCs)
- biomolecular modeling and molecular simulation
- AI and machine learning for molecular science
- scientific communication
